# Credits and licenses for bundled theme media

Every photo and video below comes from the **NASA Image and Video Library**
(<https://images.nasa.gov>, API: <https://images-api.nasa.gov>). Each item's credit line is NASA or
NASA/JPL-Caltech only; items credited to ESA, partner agencies or individual photographers were not
used.

**License.** NASA media is generally not subject to copyright in the United States, so it is in the
public domain. See the
[NASA Media Usage Guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/). Use must not
imply NASA's endorsement. Credit is given here as a courtesy.

The files in the repo are re-encoded copies: images scaled to 2560 px on the long side (never
upscaled) as JPEG at quality 84 to 88, the video transcoded to 1920x1080 H.264 with no audio, and the
`preview.jpg` files made from them (or captured from the running wallpaper). The originals are not
kept.

## Original to this repo

`aurora/aurora.wgsl`, `midnight/midnight.wgsl` and `webdemo/index.html` are shaders and a page written
for mypaper, with no third-party media. `app-example/preview.jpg` and the other `preview.jpg` files
for shader themes were rendered from them.
